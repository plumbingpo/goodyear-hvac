The second is a furnace that keeps cycling on and off or produces a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and call us. That small step can save you from a much larger repair.

Keeping Your Glendale HVAC System Running Longer

Most mid-season breakdowns start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and much easier on you than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

Small problems rarely stay small for long. Consider a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a quick, affordable repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a few miserable nights.

Comfort Challenges Glendale Homeowners Face

Most local HVAC failures announce themselves before they happen. A breaker that trips every few days, a condenser that hums louder each month, rooms drifting further from the thermostat setting, a musty smell at startup. Those are early signs of failing capacitors, refrigerant leaks, dirty coils, and duct problems, and caught early they're a quick visit. Ignored, they take out compressors and heat exchangers, the two most expensive parts in the system. We find the root cause instead of treating symptoms, so small problems stay small.

How Mesa Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Routine maintenance is the difference between a system that runs for years and one that quits in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so small issues get caught early.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners last 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and far less stressful than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Take a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a short, affordable repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a few miserable nights.
